The IEEE GRSS (Geoscience and Remote Sensing Society) Student Chapter of Manipal University Jaipur conducted an
online workshop on “Journey from Development to Production” as part of its academic and community engagement
initiatives. The event was held on 22nd December, 2025, in an online mode.
We had the honor of hosting Mr. Pratyush Kumar Deka as our distinguished speaker. The session brought together
students, faculty members, and dignitaries to discuss industry based learning in DevOps and cloud platforms
