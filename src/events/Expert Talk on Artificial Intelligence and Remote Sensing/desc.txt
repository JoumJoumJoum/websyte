The IEEE GRSS (Geoscience and Remote Sensing Society) Student Chapter, Manipal University Jaipur,
organized a Guest Talk Session as part of its academic and community engagement initiatives. The event
was held on 18th September 2025 in the School of Computer Science and Engineering.

We had the honor of hosting Dr. Surendra Kumar Sharma, Scientist at the Indian Institute of Remote
Sensing (IIRS), Dehradun, as our distinguished speaker. The session brought together students, faculty
members, and dignitaries to explore the applications of Artificial Intelligence and Remote Sensing in
real-world contexts
